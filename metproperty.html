A property marketplace that brings search, information and professional services into one place.

Brand identity, product UI and banner system

The Scenario

Buying a property is not just about finding a house. People often have to search across different platforms, compare details, check financing options and find the right professionals — all while trying to work out what to do next. That made the journey feel fragmented. Someone might find a property they like, then leave the platform to handle the next step somewhere else.



What if the whole property journey could happen in one place?

That was the starting point for MetProperty: one connected place for property search, useful information and professional support.

The Solution

I designed MetProperty as a connected property platform. Users can search for homes, understand the key details and connect with the right professionals without jumping between different websites. The goal was simple: make it easier to move from finding a property to knowing what to do next. Below shows the user flow for the proposed solution.

My Role
Banner Design

I created a flexible banner system for property listings, campaigns and seasonal moments. The layouts can change from one campaign to another, but the typography, spacing and overall MetProperty feel stay consistent.

Design Considerations

I focused on three things: helping people find the right property, making key information easy to understand, and showing them what they can do next.

  • Easy Navigation
  • Clear Information
  • Search & Filter
  • Connected Journey
Final Design

The final design makes property discovery feel more focused. Important details are easy to scan, while the next step is always clear — whether that means viewing a listing, comparing options or contacting a professional.

My Role

I handled the visual design for the project, including the logo, colours, UI, layouts and campaign banners. I also built a banner system that could adapt to different property types and seasonal campaigns without losing the MetProperty identity.

Conclusion

The project brings the main parts of the property journey together and gives users a clearer path from browsing to taking action.

What I Learned
  • The journey matters more than the listing People don't just want to find a house — they want to know what to do next. Bringing search, info, and professionals into one place is what actually helps.
  • Banner systems need to breathe I made a banner system that works across festivals, property types, and campaigns. The trick was keeping the identity consistent without making every banner look the same.
  • Less info, more confidence I kept going back and forth on how much property detail to show. Turns out, giving people just enough to feel confident about the next step works better than dumping everything on them.
