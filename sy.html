The Project

Shuang Ying is an online gaming platform covering live casino, slots and sports betting. I worked on the website revamp from start to finish, including wireframes, UI design, responsive layouts and promotional banners for web and mobile.

My Role

Wireframing, UI design and banner design across web and mobile.

My Role
Website Revamp

I redesigned the website and mobile experience, covering wireframes, UI, responsive layouts and promotional banners.

My Role
Banner design

I created campaign banners based on the visual direction for each promotion. I started by reviewing the available assets, then selected and combined the visuals that best supported the message.

What I Learned
  • Banners are tiny stories Every banner has about two seconds to communicate something. I learned how to use composition, colour, and imagery to get a message across in a single glance — no reading required.
  • Designing for two screens at once Redesigning for web and app at the same time taught me how the same content needs to adapt. Layout, navigation, banners — they all behave differently on a phone versus a desktop, and you can't just shrink one to fit the other.
  • Wireframes first, always I started with wireframes before touching any visual design. It's tempting to jump straight into making things look good, but getting the structure right first is what makes the final product actually work.
