A platform that helps homeowners find renovation professionals and ideas

My Role

Designed the website structure, user journey and key renovation features.

Design: Logo, colors, typography

Problem

Finding the right renovation professional can be slow and uncertain.

Solution

One place to discover and compare designers and contractors.

Project Overview

1 PLATFORM

A simpler renovation journey

Renof connects homeowners with renovation professionals, services and useful resources in one place.

Renof helps homeowners discover designers, contractors, renovation ideas and useful resources as they plan a renovation.

Who It's For

Renovation is already stressful. I designed for two groups who need very different things from the platform.

🏠
Homeowners
People looking for a reliable designer or contractor. They need clear information, realistic pricing and an easy way to compare options.
🛠
Professionals
Designers and contractors who want to show their work and attract the right clients. They need a profile that feels more useful than a basic listing.
Design Direction

I kept the design clean and approachable. A structured layout and consistent visual language make the platform feel professional without feeling cold.

UI/UX Design

I used clear navigation, structured content and repeatable UI patterns to make browsing feel simple and predictable.

Key Features

The platform gives users one place to explore professionals, view their work and compare options for their renovation project.

Final Outcome

The final website brings Renof’s professionals, services and renovation content together in one clear platform.

What I Learned
  • You're designing for two sides at once Homeowners and professionals want totally different things. The hard part isn't designing for one — it's making sure neither side feels like an afterthought.
  • Finding the right person is the real problem Most renovation platforms focus on inspiration. But the actual bottleneck is discovery — finding a reliable professional. That's where I spent most of my effort.
  • Clean doesn't mean boring I wanted it to feel approachable, not corporate. A clean layout with a structured system still has personality if you get the details right.
