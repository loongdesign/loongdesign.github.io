The Context

I worked on this during my internship at IQ Trivia. The idea is simple: answer trivia questions on your phone and win real cash. I built the visual identity from scratch, including the logo, colours, typography, animations and UI. It was a useful project because I got to work across the whole product.

What I Learned
  • Building a brand from nothing I had to create the logo, colours, and typography from zero. That teaches you fast how all the pieces have to work together — change one colour and suddenly the whole identity shifts.
  • Animation isn't just decoration I used to think of animation as the cherry on top. After this project, I realised it's actually how you guide attention and give feedback. The right motion can make an app feel responsive without adding a single word.
  • Phone-first, always Designing for a trivia app on a phone screen taught me that touch targets, visual hierarchy on small screens, and instant feedback aren't optional — they're the difference between an app that feels good and one that feels cheap.
