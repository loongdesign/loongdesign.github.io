B9Casino — Online Casino Platform for Singapore

My Role

Website prototype based on wireframes

Design: Logo, colors, typography

Introduction

B9Casino is an online casino platform in Singapore covering live casino, slots, sports betting, esports and 4D. I worked on the website direction and campaign visuals, using football imagery and stadium backgrounds to give the brand a more energetic feel.

The Goal

The goal was to make B9Casino feel more recognisable in a crowded market. It needed to feel exciting without losing trust, and the banner system had to work across seasonal campaigns and sports events.

Why This Direction

I chose a sports-led direction because football and live events were central to the audience. Stadium backgrounds and match-day imagery gave the platform the energy people expect from sports betting.

The visual approach changed with each campaign. Chinese New Year and Christmas used festive colours and motifs, while EPL and Euro 2020 used match-day imagery and prediction visuals. The banners looked different, but they still felt like the same brand.
